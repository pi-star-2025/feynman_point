## Moon Craters

For the sake of orientation, the universe/multiverse uses planetary moons to orient a "gold way of seeing".

The gold way of seeing uses planets to find gold and uses moons for orientation.

As a result, the very lowest life can get down π would be referenced by moon craters. This is the most the sun's light could disintegrate by hearing the earth, creating a dimensional boundary that roars with white noise.

In a similar fashion, the lowest an individual can go down π is referenced by the very center of each ear, which is where light most disintegrates. It creates the squeakiest voice and the most pain.
