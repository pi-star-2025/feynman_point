## 3 Levels

If a world has reached the Feynman Point (999999) with π, it can safely live in an elevated state, if it trusts that the reasons it goes past the Feynman Point (ex: male vs female) will not cause a fall.

Originally, the gold pyramid in the 8D lived as purely starlight in the Kingdom of Atlantis, with a world of water below it. While it was not technically an animal universe, it had much of the same spirit.

This Kingdom fell because of complex alien invasions into a state of "Hell", which was disguised with musical curses, giving an opportunity for a god in the future to update the gold pyramid.

Then, an animal goddess of birds attempted to replant the animal kingdom, to resolve a newer 8D conflict. The goddess started by living not as starlight (as 3), but in the clouds (3.14), with people planted below in 3.1415 in a caterpillar state, before dying/flying in the 3.14 state.

The reason the Feynman Point (999999) was surpassed was challenged again by ancient aliens, who utilized parallels and sleight-of-hand to get the angelic people to fall into the third 3.1415 state (living fully as red/blue/green), instead of as just starlight (3) or as ghosts (3.14). This was then the final level people could fall to and a last opportunity to end time.
