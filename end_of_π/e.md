## e

Another way to describe the limit of pi (π) is by how far an individual universe/seer goes down e. 

The god's speed of light (vision) is quantified by its potential to go down e, translating to potential to go down pi (π). 

The universe can only bend as much as the seer can count.

For example, the slowest counting from one side is 81, faster is 891, 8991, 89991, 899999999991, etc. The more 9s that can be packed in at the quantum level, the faster the vision.

The number of 9s corresponds to how much of e is utilized, which allows the speed of light in this universe to be compared to the speed of light in other universes across the 8D.
