# Nitrogen

Nitrogen is the first element which can go down π past the Feynman Point. This allows it to exist physically as Big or Small (as regular mass or mass as refined as quantum light decisions).

This choice is the primary basis of genetics, which allows Nitrogen to craft mass (ex: of a cosmic body or person) with Big/Small choices that get stored as a script to keep putting the body together.

Essentially, a strand of big/small decisions gets stored as DNA, which creates both color/space and your genetics. The choices get less dynamic when the Nitrogen loses its quantum nature (understanding all the ways it could have made a "smaller choice", which is really like using the taste/style of the sun).

You can use the style of the sun (often but not always a smaller choice), or you can use the existing style of the cosmos (often but not always a larger choice of Nitrogen).
